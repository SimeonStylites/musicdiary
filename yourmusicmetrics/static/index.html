Your Music Metrics

Upload your Spotify Extended Streaming History to see some metrics.

  1. Go to spotify.com/account/privacy and sign in.
  2. Scroll down to Download your data. Tick Extended streaming history only. Leave Account data and Technical log information unticked. Then click Request next to the selected item.
  3. Spotify will immediately send you an email asking to confirm your request. Open that email and click the confirmation link — the archive is not prepared until you do. 3–4 days later a second email arrives with the download link. Save the ZIP — it contains files like Streaming_History_Audio_2025_1.json.
  4. Drop the ZIP (or a few of those JSON files) below.
